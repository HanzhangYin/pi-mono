000031	their indices are

000086	- claim-2: The Pieri enumeration and reduction to the ten rows are mathematically plausible and cover the three boundary regimes. However, the proof is conditional on an ungrounded ambient presentation. In particular, the assertion that the interval Schur span is an ideal needs the quotient Littlewood–Richardson rule and the argument \(c_{\lambda\mu}^{\nu}\ne0\Rightarrow\mu\subseteq\nu\), using LR symmetry. The direct-sum equality also requires integral linear independence of the in-box Schur classes. These facts are neither proved nor supported by claim-1. The homogeneous reduction should explicitly replace \(a_\ell m\) by its degree-\((d+4-\ell)\) component and use \(M_t=0\) for \(t<d\). Thus the requested target is established only conditionally on the stated integral box-Schur/LR/Pieri presentation, not unconditionally. No prohibited determinant, higher-degree, or minimality conclusion is made.
