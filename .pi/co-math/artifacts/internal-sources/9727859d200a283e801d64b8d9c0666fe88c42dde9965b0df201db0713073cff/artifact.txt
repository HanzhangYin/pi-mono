000019	## Strategy
000020	Let \(B_{r,q}=\{\lambda:\lambda\subseteq q^r\}\). The Schur-basis box definition is
000021	\[
000022	I_{r,q}=\bigoplus_{\lambda\notin B_{r,q}}\mathbb Zs_\lambda.
000023	\]
000024	Since
000025	\[
000026	(i+1)^{j+1}\subseteq(i+p)^{j+c}
000027	\qquad(p,c\ge1),
000028	\]
000029	one has \(B_{j+1,i+1}\subseteq B_{j+c,i+p}\). Taking complements gives
000030	\[
000031	\{\lambda:\lambda\not\subseteq(i+p)^{j+c}\}
000032	 \subseteq
000033	\{\lambda:\lambda\not\subseteq(i+1)^{j+1}\},
000034	\]
000035	which proves the ideal inclusion.
000036	
000037	Thus there is a quotient map
000038	\[
000039	\pi:\Lambda/I_{j+c,i+p}\twoheadrightarrow\Lambda/I_{j+1,i+1}.
000040	\]
000041	Under the Schur-basis definition,
000042	\[
000043	J_{p,c}=\bigoplus_{i^j\subseteq\lambda\subseteq(i+p)^{j+c}}
000044	\mathbb Z\bar s_\lambda ,
000045	\qquad
000046	J_{1,1}=\bigoplus_{i^j\subseteq\lambda\subseteq(i+1)^{j+1}}
000047	\mathbb Z\bar s_\lambda .
000048	\]
000049	For a basis element of \(J_{p,c}\), \(\pi(\bar s_\lambda)\) is \(\bar s_\lambda\) if
000050	\(\lambda\subseteq(i+1)^{j+1}\), and is \(0\) otherwise. Hence its image is exactly \(J_{1,1}\), proving the stated restriction is surjective.
000051	
000052	Finally \(\pi\) is \(\Lambda\)-linear, so
000053	\[
000054	\pi(\Lambda_+J_{p,c})=\Lambda_+\pi(J_{p,c})
000055	=\Lambda_+J_{1,1}.
000056	\]
000057	Therefore \(\pi|_{J_{p,c}}\) descends to the asserted surjection on \(Q\).
