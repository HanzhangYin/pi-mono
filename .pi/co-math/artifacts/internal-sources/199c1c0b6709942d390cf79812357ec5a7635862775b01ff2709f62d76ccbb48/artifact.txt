000023	## Strategy
000024	Write \(\rho=(\rho _1,\ldots,\rho_a)\), padded by zeros, and
000025	\[
000026	\rho'_k=\#\{\ell: \rho_\ell\ge k\}\qquad(1\le k\le b),
000027	\]
000028	also padded by zeros. Thus
000029	\[
000030	L_\rho=(i^j,\rho),\qquad
000031	R_\rho=(i+\rho'_1,\ldots,i+\rho'_b,i^{j-b})
000032	\]
000033	are partitions: \(\rho_1\le b\le i\), \(\rho'_1\le a\), \(b\le j\), and the parameter inequalities put both in \(q^r\).
000034	
000035	The full-coordinate Hopf descent, equivalently the antipode/Cauchy–Binet expansion with
000036	\(S(s_\mu)=(-1)^{|\mu|}s_{\mu'}\), gives the integral rectangular-straightening identity
000037	\[
000038	s_{L_\rho}-(-1)^{|\rho|}s_{R_\rho}
000039	 =\sum_{\sigma\subsetneq\rho}F_{\rho,\sigma}s_{L_\sigma},
000040	\qquad
000041	F_{\rho,\sigma}\in\Lambda_{|\rho|-|\sigma|}. \tag{1}
000042	\]
000043	Here every coefficient has strictly positive degree. This is the full-coordinate form: no \(\theta\)-coordinates are discarded. It follows by expanding the Hopf identity in the Schur basis; the two endpoint terms are \(L_\rho\) and \(R_\rho\), and every other term has lower attachment \(\sigma\subsetneq\rho\). The endpoint sign is exactly the antipode sign \((-1)^{|\rho|}\).
000044	
000045	After projection to \(A\), each \(\bar s_{L_\sigma}\) lies in \(J_v\), while
000046	\(\bar F_{\rho,\sigma}\in A_+\). Hence every summand on the right of (1) lies in \(A_+J_v\). Passing to \(Q\) proves (E). The accepted graded-kernel presentation ensures that these are equalities in the integral quotient \(Q\), with no torsion or unrecorded kernel ambiguity.
000047	
000048	Boundary cases: if \(\rho=\varnothing\), then \(L_\rho=R_\rho=i^j\), so (E) is the identity. If \(a=0\), this is the only \(\rho\). Zero parts in \(\rho\) and \(\rho'\) are omitted from displayed partitions.
