000005	- [proved] **Block lemma.** Let \(M\in\mathbb Z^{R\times C}\), with finite ordered sets \(C=B\sqcup D\), and let \(P\subseteq R\), \(|P|=|D|\). Put
000006	  \[
000007	  A=M[P,B],\qquad U=M[P,D]\in GL_{|D|}(\mathbb Z).
000008	  \]
000009	  If each \(r\in R\setminus P\) satisfies
000010	  \[
000011	  M[r,B]=M[r,D]U^{-1}A,
000012	  \]
000013	  then the rows indexed by \(P\) generate the complete row lattice of \(M\). The Smith diagonal of \(M\) has exactly \(|D|\) nonzero entries, all equal to \(1\), and \(\mathbb Z^C/\operatorname{row}_{\mathbb Z}(M)\) is freely generated by the images of the \(B\)-coordinate vectors.

000015	## Strategy
000016	Write selected rows as \([A\ U]\).  For \(r\notin P\), set
000017	\[
000018	x_r=M[r,D]U^{-1}\in\mathbb Z^{1\times |D|},
000019	\]
000020	which is integral since \(U^{-1}\in\mathbb Z^{|D|\times|D|}\). The residual identity gives
000021	\[
000022	M[r,:]=[M[r,B]\ M[r,D]]
000023	      =[x_rA\ x_rU]
000024	      =x_r[A\ U].
000025	\]
000026	Thus every row is in the lattice generated by the selected rows; the reverse containment is immediate.
000027	
000028	Define the unimodular column transformation
000029	\[
000030	W=\begin{pmatrix}
000031	I_{|B|}&0\\
000032	-U^{-1}A&U^{-1}
000033	\end{pmatrix}\in GL_{|C|}(\mathbb Z).
000034	\]
000035	Then
000036	\[
000037	[A\ U]W=[0\ I_{|D|}].
000038	\]
000039	Every nonselected row transforms to \([0\ x_r]\), hence integral row operations subtracting \(x_r\) times the selected rows reduce \(MW\) to \([0\ I_{|D|}]\), with possible zero rows. Therefore its Smith diagonal, and hence that of \(M\), has \(|D|\) unit entries and no other nonzero entries.
000040	
000041	For the stated original \(B\)-classes, define
000042	\[
000043	\pi:\mathbb Z^B\oplus\mathbb Z^D\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^B,\qquad
000044	\pi(b,d)=b-dU^{-1}A.
000045	\]
000046	Its kernel is precisely
000047	\[
000048	\{x[A\ U]:x\in\mathbb Z^{1\times |D|}\}
000049	=\operatorname{row}_{\mathbb Z}(M).
000050	\]
000051	Since \(\pi(e_b)=e_b\) for each \(b\in B\), it induces an isomorphism
000052	\[
000053	\mathbb Z^C/\operatorname{row}_{\mathbb Z}(M)\cong\mathbb Z^B,
000054	\]
000055	with basis given by the images of the \(B\)-coordinate vectors.
000056	
000057	Boundary cases are included: if \(D=\varnothing\), then \(U=I_0\), the hypothesis forces all rows to vanish, and the quotient is \(\mathbb Z^B\); if \(B=\varnothing\), the quotient is \(0\).
