000041	## Critique
000042	- claim-1: The partition enumeration and unitriangular minors are mathematically correct once the relevant presentation is assumed. However, the argument silently identifies “degree-\(d+k\) columns” with Schur classes \(s_\mu\) satisfying \(i^j\subseteq\mu\subseteq(i+1)^{j+1}\). An integral box Schur basis alone does not supply this column-indexing statement or establish that all \(h_1\)-rows arise by multiplying every degree-\(d+k-1\) column. These presentation facts must be explicitly assumed, derived, or grounded. The Pieri argument should also explicitly exclude every other addable box. Thus the strategy proves the requested combinatorial result conditionally, but the requested target is not established unconditionally or from the stated hypotheses alone.
