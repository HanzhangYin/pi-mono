000022	Since \(|\lambda'|=|\lambda|\), \(\Omega\) is graded. Therefore it maps the positive-degree ideal onto the positive-degree ideal:
000023	\[
000024	\Omega(A_+)=A^\vee_+.
000025	\]
000026	Consequently,
000027	\[
000028	\Omega(A_+J_v)=\Omega(A_+)\Omega(J_v)
000029	=A^\vee_+J_{v^\vee}.
000030	\]
000031	Thus \(\Omega|_{J_v}\) descends to an integral graded isomorphism
000032	\[
000033	\bar\Omega:J_v/A_+J_v\overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow}
000034	J_{v^\vee}/A^\vee_+J_{v^\vee}.
000035	\]
000036	
000037	For every degree \(d\), its restriction is an isomorphism of finitely generated abelian groups
000038	\[
000039	\bar\Omega_d:(J_v/A_+J_v)_d\overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow}
000040	(J_{v^\vee}/A^\vee_+J_{v^\vee})_d.
000041	\]
000042	Hence it restricts to an isomorphism on integral torsion subgroups and induces equality of free ranks:
000043	\[
000044	\operatorname{Tor}(Q_d)\cong\operatorname{Tor}(Q^\vee_d),
000045	\qquad
000046	\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}Q_d=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}Q^\vee_d.
000047	\]
000048	Therefore every certified degreewise indecomposables computation transfers unchanged, including torsion factors and ranks, to the conjugate case.
