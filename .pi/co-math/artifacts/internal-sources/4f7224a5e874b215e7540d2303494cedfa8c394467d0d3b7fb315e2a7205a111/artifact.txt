000030	\[
000031	\omega(s_{\rho/\sigma})=s_{\rho'/\sigma'}

000099	## Critique
000100	- claim-1: Not established. Equation (3) is asserted rather than obtained from a displayed bordered matrix. Its factors have sizes \(b\) and \(j+a\), so an ordinary Laplace expansion would require a matrix of size \(j+a+b\), yet only the target \(j\times j\) Jacobi–Trudi matrix is displayed. No row sets, column sets, complementary minors, vanishing-minor argument, or reduction of the bordered determinant to the target determinant is supplied. Consequently the claimed “fixed border” sign \((-1)^{|\rho|}\) is unsupported; the full Laplace sign \((-1)^{\sum R+\sum C}\) was not computed. Moreover, the stated hypotheses allow \(b>i\), when \((i^j,\sigma)\) need not be a partition. For example, \(i=1,j=2,a=1,b=2,\rho=(2)\) produces \((1,1,2)\). Either \(b\le i\) must be assumed, as in the intended \(b=\min(i,j)\) setting, or generalized Jacobi–Trudi functions for integer sequences must be explicitly defined.
000101	- claim-2: The observations for \(\rho=\varnothing\) and \(a=0\) are correct with the \(0\times0\) determinant convention, but they do not repair claim-1. For \(b=j\), the trailing block is indeed empty, but if ordinary Schur notation is intended one still needs \(j=b\le i\).
000102	- claim-3: Not established as written because claim-1 remains unproved. In addition, \(J_v\to J_v/A_+J_v\) is not a quotient map defined on arbitrary elements of \(A\); one must first prove every displayed term lies in \(J_v\). The actual quotient consequence was omitted: for every proper \(\sigma\subsetneq\rho\), \(\omega(s_{\rho/\sigma})\) has positive degree, so its product lies in \(A_+J_v\), and the projected identity should reduce to
000103	  \[
000104	  [\bar s_{(i^j,\rho)}]
000105	  =(-1)^{|\rho|}
000106	  [\bar s_{(i+\rho'_1,\ldots,i+\rho'_b,i^{j-b})}].
000107	  \]
000108	  No assertion that \(\omega\) itself descends to \(A\) is justified unless the relevant rectangle is preserved.
